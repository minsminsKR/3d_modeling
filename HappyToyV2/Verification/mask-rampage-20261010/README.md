# Mask rampage and Korean Baby voice

The corridor Mask releases as an already-grown runner after the third memory.
Patrol is 6.6m/s and chase is 7.2m/s. Real nearby route crossings shatter ordinary
movable doors, including locked and already-open leaves. Their removed leaf
collision and navigation obstacle remain removed across the production checkpoint
round trip. Exit seals remain their separate existing interaction kind. Close
7m actual physical sight rapidly admits a chase, while opaque room cover and
occupied cabinets retain the existing protection.

Blender 4.0.2 authored a volumetric crooked body, hollow porcelain mask, deep
open sockets/mouth, irregular teeth and recessed eyes. Editable multires sculpt,
voxel anatomy, actual 2K Cycles PBR bakes, two `.blend` sources and a body LOD are
in `SourceArt/MaskHorror`; their hashes and reproduction commands accompany them.
These are original procedural/sculpted fictional surfaces, not a scan or
photogrammetry. Unity fits only the authored visual children beneath the original
rig, body and mask pivots. Actual geometry establishes attachment bounds, and
original navigation root/capsule, running clip and eye ownership are retained.

Heavy contact, close human whistle and wood impact cues use retained licensed
recordings. Baby searching movement admits two young-character Korean generated
voice variants saying “어디..? 어디있어??”; a paused, stationary or chasing Baby
does not start searching speech. The voice briefly ducks the existing cry and
owns its own imported-resource copies. Source recordings, original neural voice
candidates, candidate selection, pinned generators and licenses are committed.
Independent unprompted offline ASR recognizes both prepared Korean cues. This
is generated character speech, not a real child recording.

Evidence here includes targeted PlayMode results, native controlled fixtures,
actual player-camera rendering, asset/hash validation, ASR and natural listener
DSP statistics. The native Mask fixture uses production third-memory release,
real navigation, actual ordinary locked/open doors, actual cabinet use, pause
and checkpoint APIs. Its opaque cover counterfactual is an explicit fixture.
The Baby fixture uses a valid production post-chase checkpoint restore and two
local supported patrol markers beside an occupied real cabinet, with other
sources muted. These validate specific mechanics, visuals and mix/lifecycle
contracts, rather than a complete naturally played survival route.

Both final native players ran with `-quiet-diagnostics`. Windows current-process
device-session mute was verified before positive pre-device Unity DSP capture.
Shutdown returned listener gain to zero, restored the original device-session
mute and restored user preferences. Diagnostic WAVs and verbose machine logs
remain optional local evidence outside the repository; the retained hashes and
statistics identify them. No speaker playback or human listening certification
was performed. Neither frame rate nor subjective fear is certified by these
fixtures. Normal game launches retain the user's audio settings.
