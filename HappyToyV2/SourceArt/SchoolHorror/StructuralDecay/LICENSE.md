# Primary scan rights

The acquired original `.blend` files and all their referenced original maps are
Poly Haven CC0 1.0 assets. Their primary asset pages and official licence are:

- Concrete Debris — Amal Kumar: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_debris
- Namaqualand Stones 01 — Greg Zaal (photography), Jenelle van Heerden (modeling):
  https://polyhaven.com/a/namaqualand_stones_01
- Asset licence: https://polyhaven.com/license
- CC0 legal code: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

Poly Haven explicitly permits use, modification and redistribution of the assets,
including distribution with editable project sources. No external branding,
preview photographs, artist avatars or site artwork is included.

`source-manifest.json` preserves official download URLs, API checksums, authors,
source kind, original dimensions and actual downloaded SHA-256 hashes.

Concrete Debris is a photographed PBR/height scan, not a volumetric rubble model.
The game rubble relief is derived from that actual height field. The second asset
contains genuine scanned individual quartz stones and is used as small exposed
aggregate. It is not described as scanned Korean school plaster. Architectural
plaster shell edges and lath are authored geometry layered around real openings.
