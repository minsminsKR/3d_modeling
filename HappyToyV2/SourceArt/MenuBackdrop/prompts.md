Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production horror-game title and pause-menu background wallpaper, landscape 16:9, ideally 2048x1152 or higher.

Preserve these character traits and the unique single-eye anatomy; absolutely no second eye or flanking red dots.

Near-black charcoal background; only faint indistinct abandoned corridor shadows, no distracting props or extra characters. Rich photographic texture without bright oversaturated flat red rings.
Constraints: exactly one eye in the centre of this creature's face, original pupil unobscured, faithful recognizable Cyclopse identity, much scarier expression, no UI, no letters, no text, no logos, no watermark, no frame, no literal gore or detached body parts, no neon outlines, no second eye. Non-transparent background.
