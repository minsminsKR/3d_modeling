# Eye mask and reused asset provenance

The anatomical region definitions, source measurement helpers and UV-rasterized
grayscale emission masks are original project authoring code/data. They introduce
no third-party images or models. Original eye albedos are neither edited nor
redistributed as new textures; the renderer continues to use the original project
assets and their established rights/provenance. Original refinement .blend files,
static FBX models, skin materials and source textures remain unchanged.

Existing forged door hardware is the project's original Blender asset at
`SourceArt/GraphicsUpgrade/Props/door-hardware.blend`, with generator
`SourceArt/GraphicsUpgrade/author_props_v3.py`. Reused rust PBR scan is
**rusty_metal_02**, Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven, CC0 1.0; original hashes/attribution/
license remain in `ThirdParty/Graphics/polyhaven-pbr-manifest.json` and
`ThirdParty/Graphics/Graphics-Credits.txt`. Existing brass/cloth/paper materials
retain their original procedural source records. No new asset download is needed.
