# Village dusk cloud panorama

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-12. The original `clouds-original.png` is copied byte-for-byte to `Assets/Resources/AlleyDusk/clouds.png`; SHA-256 `84383ab5a8fd584cce9bb12d8fc64edb90b34df70f68d810a33526f7eeaa3968`.

Prompt: Use case: photorealistic game sky texture. A seamless horizontally wrapping equirectangular 360 degree panorama of a real Korean neighborhood dusk SKY ONLY, no ground. Broad naturally layered sunset clouds, fine wispy turbulent details and subtle atmospheric haze, late evening after sunset. Color near horizon muted salmon and dusty mauve, upper sky subdued deep blue gray and violet. Realistic photographic exposure and texture, understated low saturation, authentic tranquil slightly ominous atmosphere. Panorama is intended to be wrapped around the player, align the clouds and colors at the left and right edges seamlessly, with a wide horizon in the lower part and sky overhead. No buildings, trees, mountains, landscape, moon, sun disc, stars, electrical wires, letters, borders, watermark, dramatic fantasy clouds, lightning or oversaturated orange.

The generated image is a horizon-to-zenith sky, not a full spherical HDRI. `AlleyDuskSky.shader` maps it by world direction, blends its horizontal seam, fades the uppermost region to the existing procedural sky and retains the authored night-sky transition. `AlleyDuskEnvironment` supplies the image; when absent the original procedural dusk remains. Copy the original and run Unity editor method `HappyToy.V2.Editor.AlleyDuskCloudImport.Apply` to reproduce import settings on this file only.

The focused D3D11 village camera reviewed forward, rear, wrap and overhead directions. A test moves the camera to sample the transition midpoint and the restored mountain sky; this is a diagnostic camera reposition, not a full traversal. The images are static samples; no standalone build or frame-rate measurement was performed. The image is not an HDRI lighting source and the distant scene still needs visual improvement.
