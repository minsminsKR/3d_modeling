# Cyclopse design — user direction, 2026-10-12

Use user-reference-round-cyclopse.png as the character identity reference.
The user requests this round furry creature, standing and chasing rather than
rolling/crawling, with an uncanny-valley feeling. Preserve the bulbous tawny fur,
plausibly human face/skin, one central eye, dark short beard/moustache and flower.
Use short articulated legs for upright locomotion; avoid a generic muscular bear
body or replacing its face with a cartoon eye. Keep the face visible in motion.
Generate the 3D base with the designated A100 Studio webapp; preserve its job
provenance, original output and editable authoring source. Rig and animate the
actual generated anatomy for upright patrol/chase and the existing school intro.
